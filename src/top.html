      Lessons

      Go Live

      Turn on live data (free)

      1. Unzip market-desk.zip. Make a free account at github.com, create a repository, click uploading an existing file, and drag in everything inside the folder.
      2. Sign up at vercel.com with GitHub. Click Add New → Project, pick the repository, and click Deploy.
      3. Open your Vercel link. The pill at the top turns green and says Live. Prices, charts, earnings and news then refresh every 5 minutes.
      4. Or hand the zip to your developer: running npx vercel in the folder does all of this.

      Data comes from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints through your own proxy. It is free and needs no key, but it is unofficial and can change or rate-limit. Futures prices are delayed about 10 minutes. Good for scanning, not for execution.
